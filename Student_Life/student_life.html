STUDENT LIFE

Growing together.
Learning beyond the classroom.

At Gyan Bharti Public School, students learn through participation, friendship, discipline, creativity and experiences that extend far beyond the classroom.

01
LIFE AT GYAN BHARTI

More than lessons.
A community.

Student life brings together learning, movement, creativity and meaningful relationships. Every experience gives students an opportunity to discover their interests and grow in confidence.

From the playing field to hostel life and co-curricular activities, students are encouraged to participate, cooperate and make the most of their school years.

02 / SPORTS
SPORTS & FITNESS

Strength of body.
Strength of character.

Sports provide students with opportunities to stay active, develop discipline and experience the value of teamwork. Participation on the field teaches students to work together, respect others and keep challenging themselves.
