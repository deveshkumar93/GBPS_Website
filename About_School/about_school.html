ABOUT SCHOOL

Education with a purpose.

Created with a vision to provide quality education to all children of the society at an affordable cost. GYAN BHARTI PUBLIC SCHOOL have carved out a niche for itself and stand as a premier institution of learning today.

The Bithnauli campus is spread over an estate of 5 acre in a pollution free, calm and lush green atmosphere conducive to learning and free thinking. The school doesnot focus on one element in isolation, rather a whole child approach to education is taken.

The school provides appropriate facilities, managed classroom and a clear, fair disciplinary policy. The school provides education where the focus is on formation of character, increasing the strength of mind, broadening the intellect and making learners capable to stand on one's own foot.

The school is a true educational english medium and it also provides hostel facility.

OUR APPROACH
02

Learning
Philosophy.

Learning experiences created for learners in school, help to build knowledge, skills and attitudes. The classroom helps the learners to analyze and evaluate their experiences, learn to doubt, to question, to investigate and to think independently. The environment of the school transforms them.

Students learn best when they actively participate in the lesson or that they feel valued when teachers encourage them to share their knowledge with their peers.

LEARNING & CURRICULUM
03

The
Curriculum.

We provide education with an objective to empower students to make decision to find creative solutions and plan effective action strategies. The curriculum is designed in such a way that a child is in touch with the everchanging world trends right from the junior level to the senior level.

We constantly review, evaluate and reshape the classroom and teaching strategy to create the most appropriate education that the child deserves. The study programme for all learning level incorporates the needs and the abilities of the child with the most appropriate teaching methodology.
